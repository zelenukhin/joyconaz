Joy-Con Controls

Controls from Joy-Cons

Where to find the necessary buttons:

  • Shoulder buttons L 1 / R 7 and triggers (ZL / ZR) — on the top edge of the controllers right under your index fingers.
  • Button '−' 2 — the elongated minus sign button at the top of the left controller.
  • Button '+' 8 — the plus sign button at the top of the right controller.
  • Capture button 5 — the small square button below the arrows on the left controller.
  • Right stick 10 — the round joystick on the right controller (can be tilted in directions and pressed inwards).

Ball movement:

  • Any of the letter buttons A, B, X, Y 9 (right controller) — Start / Stop movement.
  • Arrows ◄ and ► 4 (left controller) — speed adjustment: a single press changes speed by 0.10, holding changes it smoothly.
  • Right stick tilt 10 (right/up — faster, left/down — slower) — speed change by 0.10 for each tilt from the center.

Vibration setup:

  • Buttons '−' 2 and '+' 8 — cycle vibration pattern: '−' — previous, '+' — next.
  • Arrows ▲ and ▼ 4 (left controller) — increase (▲) or decrease (▼) vibration strength by 5%.
  • Simultaneous press of L 1 + R 7 or ZL + ZR (triggers under L and R) — add the current vibration pattern to favorites (or remove from it).

Random vibration mode:

  • Right stick press 10 (inwards until it clicks) — enable / disable random mode.
  • Square Capture button 5 (left controller) — add or remove the current vibration pattern from the random list.

Settings

Settings are stored locally on this device. You can save them to a file to transfer to another computer or restore later.

Save settings — creates a joyconaz-settings.json file. In the desktop version, a save dialog will appear; in the browser, the file will be downloaded automatically.

Load settings — allows you to choose a previously saved file. Current settings will be replaced by the settings from the file, after which the application will reload.

Help

This is a quick guide on using the application. It explains how to connect Joy-Cons, set up vibration for a session, and save settings — step by step, assuming no prior experience with gaming controllers.

Connecting Joy-Cons

  • First, put both Joy-Cons in pairing mode. To do this, find the small round Sync button on the side of each Joy-Con (circled in red on the illustration). Press and hold this Sync button until the lights start flashing. If the lights do not flash, the Joy-Cons are likely discharged and need to be charged.
  • Next, connect both Joy-Cons via Bluetooth in your computer's settings — just like connecting wireless headphones.
  • Then, in the application, click 'Connect Joy-Cons'.
  • In the desktop version of the app, previously authorized controllers usually connect automatically on the next launch — no need to press the button again.

How vibration works

The app plays the selected vibration pattern when the ball on the screen touches the left or right edge of the track. The vibration is sent to the Joy-Con in the corresponding hand: at the left edge — to the left controller, at the right — to the right.

Vibration patterns and strength

  • A 'vibration pattern' is a sequence of haptic pulses played when the ball touches an edge.
  • You can adjust the vibration strength separately for each pattern. When selecting a pattern again, the app will automatically restore its previously set strength — you won't have to adjust it from scratch.
  • If a pattern hasn't been adjusted yet, the default strength is 50%.
  • The last selected vibration pattern and its strength are remembered automatically. They will be restored on the next launch — you can resume your session from where you left off.
  • The app offers 47 vibration patterns: simple single pulses, rhythmic sequences, dynamic patterns with changing strength, and Apple Watch-style haptic clicks. The strength slider scales the overall intensity of the pattern while preserving its rhythm and character.

Test vibration before session

The 'Test Vibration' button immediately plays the selected pattern on both Joy-Cons — ball movement is not required for this. If the click sound is enabled, it will also play. Use this button to evaluate the strength and feel of the vibration before starting a session with a client.

Favorite vibration patterns

If you use the same patterns more often than others, add them to favorites — this speeds up switching between sessions.

  • The 'Favorite pattern' checkbox applies to the currently selected pattern. Check the box to add the pattern to favorites, or uncheck to remove it.
  • You can do the same directly from the Joy-Cons: simultaneously press the upper L + R buttons (on the top edges of the controllers) or the lower ZL + ZR triggers.
  • Favorite patterns are marked with a ★ symbol in the list.
  • If you enable the 'Favorites only' checkbox, only favorite patterns will be available in the list and when switching with the '−' / '+' buttons.
  • If there are no favorite patterns yet, the 'Favorites only' checkbox does nothing — the list remains full.

Vibration patterns for random mode

  • The 'Use randomly' checkbox adds the current pattern to a separate list from which the app selects patterns in random mode (see below).
  • The Capture button — a small round/square button on the inner side of the left Joy-Con, near the screen — performs the same action.
  • Patterns for random mode are marked with a 🎲 symbol.
  • This list is not linked to favorites: the same pattern can be in both lists, only one, or neither — these are two independent settings.

Random mode

Random mode is useful if you want the vibration pattern to change on its own during a session rather than remaining fixed.

  • Enable the 'Random mode' checkbox to allow the app to choose a vibration pattern on each edge touch.
  • On each edge touch, the app has a 50% chance to keep the current vibration pattern, and a 50% chance to pick a different one from the 'Use randomly' list.
  • If there are multiple patterns in the list, picking the same pattern during a change is excluded: if a change happens, the pattern will definitely be different.
  • Every vibration pattern in random mode is played with the strength saved specifically for it — the vibration strength slider doesn't affect it at this moment.
  • If the random list is empty, the currently selected vibration pattern will play with the strength set by the slider.
  • Favorite patterns and the 'Favorites only' checkbox do not affect random mode in any way — it is a separate setting.
  • Random mode can be toggled by the checkbox in the app, or by pressing the right stick — the small joystick on the top part of the right Joy-Con, which can also be pressed like a button.

Keyboard controls

The main commands are duplicated on a regular computer keyboard — useful when Joy-Cons are not at hand or it's faster to press a key. Keys work on any layout, including non-English ones.

  • Space — Start / Stop ball movement.
  • Arrows ↑ / → increase movement speed, arrows ↓ / ← decrease it — just like the arrows on the left Joy-Con: a single press changes speed by 0.10, holding changes it smoothly.
  • Keys 1 / 2 / 3 (top number row and numpad) — quickly set speed to 0.8 / 1.0 / 1.2.
  • Enter — enable or disable random mode.
  • S — enable or disable the click sound.

Click sound

The click sound can be enabled as an additional cue — it indicates which side the touch happened on, even without looking at the screen.

  • Check the 'Enable' box to play a short click sound along with the vibration.
  • Select the desired click option in the dropdown list.
  • When touching the left edge, the sound is played in the left ear; the right edge — in the right ear. This preserves the correlation between the ball's movement, sound, and vibration.
  • The sound is triggered slightly ahead of time so the click itself sounds exactly when the ball touches the edge.
  • Enabling the sound while the ball is stopped, selecting a different sound, or using the 'Test vibration' button plays a test click in the center — immediately in both speakers.
  • If the ball is already moving, enabling the checkbox won't play the test sound right away — the click will sound on the next edge touch.

Saving settings

All settings are saved on the device automatically: the selected pattern, the strength for each pattern, favorites, the list for random mode, sound settings, and checked boxes. There is no need to manually press 'save' after every session.

The 'Settings' button opens a window with 'Save settings' and 'Load settings' commands — you need them if you want to make a backup or transfer your settings to another computer.

  • 'Save settings' creates a joyconaz-settings.json file. In the desktop version, a save dialog will appear; in the browser, the file will be downloaded.
  • 'Load settings' allows you to select a previously saved file. Current settings will be replaced by the ones from the file, and the app will reload.

We recommend keeping this file in a safe place — for instance, if you work across multiple computers or reinstall your OS.
